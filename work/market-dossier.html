What it is

A market analysis for entering short-term rental management, built on official arrivals data and platform estimates - and unusually honest about the fact that the commercial data providers disagree with each other by wide margins because they each define occupancy differently. Every figure carries its source and its methodology risk, and the recommendation is to plan on the conservative band. Shipped with an owner-facing pitch page and a prospect list.

What it changes

  • Each number is labelled with the method that produced it, so nothing is quietly load-bearing
  • Sizing is stated as a band, not a single confident figure
  • Ends in something you can send: a pitch page and a list of who to send it to
