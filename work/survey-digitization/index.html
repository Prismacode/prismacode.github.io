What it is

Two further field surveys rebuilt as digital instruments: skip logic and entry guards in the form itself, searchable A-Z airport and city pickers instead of free text, and an adapter that reconstructs the exact wide column layout the downstream analysis expects. Interviewers get fewer ways to enter something wrong; the statisticians get the file they already know how to read.

What it changes

  • Bad entries are refused at the tablet, not corrected weeks later
  • Long reference lists became searchable pickers instead of typed guesses
  • Works with no signal in the field and syncs when the device is back online
