What it is

Congressional stock-transaction disclosures are public but published in a form nobody can read at a glance. This collects them, structures them, and renders a browsable dashboard of who trades what, how often, and how late the filing arrived.

What it changes

  • Six analytical views over a stream that ships as raw filings
  • Filing delay surfaced as its own measure, not buried
  • Static site: no server, no database to maintain
