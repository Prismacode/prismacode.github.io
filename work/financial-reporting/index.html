What it is

The source was a ledger typed by hand: no year on the dates, December filed after February, and categories buried in free text. The report reconciles itself against the recorded balance, shows a pass or fail on screen, and never silently drops a transaction.

What it changes

  • A visible seal when the books balance, and the exact gap when they do not
  • Nothing discarded: unrecognised entries are counted and listed to be fixed
  • Interactive on screen, clean on paper for the people who print it
