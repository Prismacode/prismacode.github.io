What it is

316 songs with keys, tempo and lyrics on every phone in the group, in a building where the connection drops. One self-contained page: nothing to install, no account, no monthly bill. The team can also add songs from inside the app instead of leaving it.

What it changes

  • A working metronome and both vocal keys on every song
