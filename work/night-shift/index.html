What it is

Long-running work is scheduled to execute overnight on the machine itself, and the result is waiting on the phone by morning. The queue stays visible so a job that failed is obvious rather than silently missing. The worker is deliberately given no delete and no edit rights: it proposes, a human applies.

What it changes

  • Hours of processing happen while you sleep
  • The outcome arrives where you already look in the morning
  • Missed runs are visible and re-runnable, not lost
